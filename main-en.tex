\chapter*{Version and use}
This open book is maintained by TopLocalAI in LaTeX, with Chinese and English editions. The current draft contains the preface, Chapters 1 and 2, a complete chapter plan, exercises, and asset provenance.

SciDraw AI provides the practical workflow used throughout the book. In this edition, Codex assists with the example figures: the data plot is rendered from a published simulated CSV by a reproducible script, and the mechanism illustration is an actual output of Codex's built-in image generation. Neither is presented as a SciDraw AI generation result. Interface screenshots show a local development version; public availability of the guide must be checked separately.
