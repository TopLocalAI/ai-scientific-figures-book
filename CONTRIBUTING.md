# Contributing / 贡献说明

1. Edit the LaTeX chapter sources. Keep section meanings and evidence boundaries aligned across Chinese and English; natural phrasing need not be literal translation.
2. Identify a teaching simulation, an abstract assumption, or real research explicitly. Include source material and permission information where needed.
3. For generated figures, retain the executed prompt, generator information actually available, original output, and a concrete review. Do not claim an output came from a platform that was not used.
4. Run make check and make. Inspect the resulting pages, especially tables, captions, and figures. Use make figures when changing the dataset or plotting code.
5. Separate editorial changes from numerical-data changes. Explain why any value or pairing changes.

By submitting a contribution, you agree to its distribution under the applicable license scope in LICENSE. Do not include credentials, private research, account data, or third-party images without appropriate permission.
