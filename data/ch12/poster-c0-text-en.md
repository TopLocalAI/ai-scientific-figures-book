## Abstract
We compare control and interface-passivated planar n-i-p perovskite solar cells for initial performance and operational stability. All curves are simulated teaching data from a single-diode model.

## Introduction
Efficiency gains in perovskite photovoltaics often trade against interfacial recombination and stability. We add a thin passivation layer at the hole-transport interface to improve open-circuit voltage and long-term retention.
